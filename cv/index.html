Research Interests

My interests lie in computer systems and compilers, with a focus on program analysis, intermediate representations, code generation and their interaction with processor architecture. Through RISC-V core design, compiler construction and CPU/GPU programming, I explore how software and hardware choices affect execution efficiency. I am particularly interested in optimizations that remain effective under practical constraints on memory, parallelism and hardware resources.
